AI Automation in Recruitment

7 tools covering
the entire hiring funnel

Stage 1

Intake & Screening

Tool No. 1

Intake Brief Assistant

A high-quality intake meeting from the very first conversation

  • Assessment of how realistic the requirements and conditions are against the market
  • Contradictions and weak spots in the profile
  • Questions and recommendations for the conversation with the hiring manager
  • Sourcing strategy: which companies employ such candidates, channels, how to sell them the role

No profile rework — the search starts in the right direction immediately.

How it's built: a prompt instruction + a structured intake template. Runs in Claude or ChatGPT, no code.

Tool No. 2

Resume Analyzer

Screening the incoming flow in minutes, not hours

  • Every resume is matched against the final job description
  • Sorted into: invite to interview / clarify experience / not a match
  • For each candidate — the reasoning: what matched, what to verify

Strong candidates don't get lost in the flow. The decision always stays with the recruiter.

How it's built: an AI project with the job description in its knowledge base + resume uploads (PDF) + a prompt with evaluation criteria.

Stage 2

Interview

Tool No. 3

Interview Prep Assistant

A personalized conversation plan for every candidate

  • Questions targeting the specific candidate's experience and skills — based on the resume analysis
  • Areas to probe: gaps, career switches, inconsistencies

A structured interview without hours of preparation. Every candidate is assessed against the same criteria.

How it's built: a prompt that takes Tool No. 2's findings and the job profile as input. Same AI project, no code.

Tool No. 4

Transcript Analyzer

A conclusion based on the whole conversation, not the interviewer's memory

  • Analysis during the interview itself: clarifying questions to ask the candidate at the end of that same meeting
  • A conclusion: strengths, risks, fit against the profile

Gaps are closed on the spot, no follow-up calls. The assessment relies on facts, not impressions.

How it's built: a call transcription service (Zoom, Meet, tl;dv, etc.) + an analysis prompt. Recording — with the candidate's consent.

Stage 3

After the Interview

Tool No. 5

Feedback & Summary Writer

Three texts after every interview — in a couple of minutes

  • For the candidate — a respectful rejection or an invitation to the next stage
  • For the hiring manager — conclusions and a recommendation
  • For the ATS — a structured record in the required format

Fast answers for candidates, clear feedback for hiring managers, complete data in the ATS.

How it's built: a prompt + email templates + a record format for the specific ATS. Takes Tool No. 4's conclusion as input.

Tool No. 6

Recruiter Performance Analyzer

A funnel retrospective: what to change in the process

  • Analysis of accumulated transcripts + outcomes: recruiter rejection / candidate withdrawal / offer declined
  • Where and why candidates are being lost
  • Specific recommendations — what to change

Growth based on your own data, not generic advice.

How it's built: a table of candidate outcomes + a transcript archive + an analytical prompt. Requires 2–3 months of data collection.

Stage 4

Trainer for Recruiters and Hiring Managers

Tool No. 7

Interview Trainer

AI plays the candidate — recruiters and hiring managers train their assessment skills

  • For recruiters — assessing soft skills and values, hard skills to a lesser extent
  • For hiring managers — practicing hard skills assessment
  • Different scenarios: a strong candidate, a borderline one, a difficult one

Interview skills grow with zero risk to real candidates. Especially valuable for hiring managers who interview rarely.

How it's built: a role-play prompt with an "AI candidate" persona and backstory + an assessment checklist. Works in text or voice mode.
